# Newton Fractal (Фрактал Ньютона)

The Newton fractal is generated by applying Newton’s method for finding roots of a complex polynomial. Each pixel is colored according to which root the iteration converges to and how fast it converges.

## Algorithm

- **Method**: Newton’s method in the complex plane.
- **Iteration**: \( z_{n+1} = z_n - f(z_n) / f'(z_n) \).
- **Polynomial**: \( f(z) = z^{\text{power}} + m \), where `power` is a uniform (default 4) and \( m \) is derived from the mouse position: \( m = 0.5 \cdot (\text{mousepos} - 0.5 \cdot (\text{width}, \text{height})) \).
- **Convergence**: Iteration stops when \( \lvert z_{n+1} - z_n \rvert < 0.001 \) or when the maximum number of iterations is reached.
- **Divergence**: If \( |z| \geq 10000 \), the point is treated as diverging and drawn black.

## Coloring

- **Hue**: Based on \( \lvert \sin(\arg z) \rvert / (8\pi) - \text{hueShift} \) at the converged \( z \).
- **Saturation**: \( 1 / \sqrt{|z|} \), clamped to 0 when \( |z| < 0.1 \).
- **Value (vibrance)**: \( \max(1 - 0.025 \cdot \text{steps}, 0) \); set to 0 when \( |z| > 100 \).
- Colors are produced in HSV and converted to RGB in the fragment shader.

## Implementation

- **Rendering**: WebGL 2; vertex shader passes clip-space positions; fragment shader runs the Newton iteration and coloring per pixel.
- **Shaders**: `public/fractals/vertex_shader.vert`, `public/fractals/fragment_shader.frag`.
- **Initialization**: `src/utils/newton-fractal.ts` loads shaders, builds the program, sets up a full-screen quad, and starts the render loop with `requestAnimationFrame`.
- **Uniforms**: `width`, `height`, `power` (default 4), `iterations`, `hueShift`, `mousepos` (for the parameter \( m \)).

## UI (Fractals page)

- **Iterations**: Slider, step 20, max 100; default 50. Controls the maximum Newton iterations per pixel.
- **Hue / color**: One of yellow (0.2), green (0.4), blue (0.6), purple (0.9), red (0.0), or “colorful” (−1). Maps to the `hueShift` uniform.
- **Mouse**: Moving the mouse changes \( m \), so the polynomial and thus the fractal shape change interactively.

## Files

- `src/utils/newton-fractal.ts` — WebGL setup, render loop, `setIterations`, `setMousePos`, `setHueShift`, `setPower`.
- `src/components/NewtonFractal.tsx` — Canvas, WebGL init, wiring of iterations and hue color to the fractal.
- `public/fractals/fragment_shader.frag` — Newton iteration and coloring.
- `public/fractals/vertex_shader.vert` — Full-screen quad.
